# Case test.1 – Test Case - Hypertension


## Case

John Smith is a 55-year-old man presenting for a routine check-up. His blood pressure is 150/95 mmHg. He has a family history of cardiovascular disease.

## Questions

<CaseQuestion
  number={1}
  question="What are the diagnostic criteria for hypertension?"
>

Hypertension is diagnosed when:
- Systolic BP ≥ 140 mmHg
- Diastolic BP ≥ 90 mmHg
- On at least two separate occasions

</CaseQuestion>

<CaseQuestion
  number={2}
  question="What lifestyle modifications would you recommend?"
>

Key lifestyle modifications include:
- Regular exercise (30 minutes daily)
- Reduced sodium intake
- Weight loss if overweight
- Limit alcohol consumption
- Smoking cessation

</CaseQuestion>
