## Case

Sarah Jones, 45 years old, presents with increased thirst and frequent urination. Fasting glucose is 8.5 mmol/L.

## Questions

<CaseQuestion
  number={1}
  question="What are the diagnostic criteria for Type 2 Diabetes?"
>

Diagnosis requires one of:
- Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 7.0 mmol/L
- Random plasma glucose ≥ 11.1 mmol/L with symptoms
- HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
- 2-hour post-OGTT glucose ≥ 11.1 mmol/L

</CaseQuestion>
